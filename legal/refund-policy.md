VoxDex ("VoxDex", "we", "us", or "our") sells Vox (our in-app virtual currency, denoted "Ꝟ" in the user interface and referred to in earlier versions of this policy and in some internal systems as "Credits") and other paid features through a third-party payment provider acting as our Merchant of Record (the "Merchant of Record"). The Merchant of Record is the seller of record for all transactions on the Service and handles billing, refunds, and payment disputes on our behalf.

Refunds for purchases made through the Merchant of Record are governed by the Merchant of Record's refund policy, which sets out the statutory withdrawal and refund rights available to consumers in different jurisdictions, as well as the Merchant of Record's discretionary refund process. The applicable policy is linked from your transaction confirmation email and from the Merchant of Record's billing portal.

## 2. How to request a refund

All refund requests are processed by the Merchant of Record. To request a refund:

- Use the "View receipt" or "Manage subscription" link in your transaction confirmation email and follow the Merchant of Record's refund process
- Or contact us at [hello@voxdex.com](mailto:hello@voxdex.com) and we will help direct your request to the Merchant of Record

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method, typically within 14 days of approval.

## 3. Statutory consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable consumer rights you have under applicable law. Where local consumer-protection law grants you stronger rights than this policy or the Merchant of Record's policy, those rights apply.

If you are a consumer in South Africa, the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 may give you additional rights, including remedies where a service is materially defective. We will honour any obligations we have under that Act.

## 4. Technical issues with the Service

If you experience a technical issue caused by VoxDex - for example, you were charged but Vox was not issued, you were charged twice, or a platform error caused the wrong package to be purchased - please contact us at [hello@voxdex.com](mailto:hello@voxdex.com). Include:

- The email associated with your VoxDex account
- The date and approximate time of the transaction
- The amount and currency
- A description of the issue
- Any transaction reference from your confirmation email

We will investigate and, where a fault on our side is confirmed, work with the Merchant of Record to issue a refund. We aim to respond within 14 days.

## 5. Chargebacks

We strongly encourage you to contact us at [hello@voxdex.com](mailto:hello@voxdex.com) or the Merchant of Record before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer - most issues are resolved more quickly directly. If a chargeback is initiated, access to your account and Vox balance may be suspended pending resolution.

## 6. Account closure

If you close your VoxDex account voluntarily, any unused Vox is forfeited at the time of closure. This does not affect any refund rights you have under the Merchant of Record's refund policy or applicable law.

If we close your account due to a violation of our [Terms of Service](/terms), unused Vox is forfeited without refund, subject to any applicable statutory rights.

If we discontinue the Service for operational reasons unrelated to any conduct on your part, we will work with the Merchant of Record to make reasonable efforts to refund unused Vox purchased in the preceding 12 months.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in effect at the time of the original purchase governs that purchase. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent change.
