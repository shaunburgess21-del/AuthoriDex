## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work when you purchase Credits or other paid features on VoxDex ("**we**", "**us**", or "**our**"), operated by **VoxDex (Pty) Ltd**. It supplements our [Terms of Service](/terms) and applies to all paid transactions made through the Service.

By purchasing Credits or any other paid feature, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this Refund Policy.

## 2. General policy: all sales are final

All purchases of Credits and other digital items are **final and non-refundable**, except where:

- A refund is required by applicable consumer-protection law (see Section 4)
- The purchase was made in error due to a verifiable technical fault on our side (see Section 3)
- We choose to grant a refund at our discretion (see Section 5)

This reflects the nature of what you are buying. Credits are a digital, virtual entitlement that becomes immediately usable on the Service the moment they are issued to your account. Once issued, they cannot be "returned" in any meaningful sense.

We make this policy clear at the point of purchase, and your transaction confirms your acceptance of it.

## 3. Refunds for technical faults

If you experience a clear technical issue caused by VoxDex that affected your purchase, you may be eligible for a refund. Examples include:

- You were charged but the Credits were never issued to your account
- You were charged twice for a single purchase
- A platform error caused you to purchase the wrong package

To request a refund in these circumstances, contact us at [hello@voxdex.com](mailto:hello@voxdex.com) within **14 days** of the transaction. Please include:

- The email address associated with your VoxDex account
- The date and approximate time of the transaction
- The amount charged and currency
- A description of the issue
- Any transaction reference or receipt provided by the payment processor

We will investigate and respond within 14 days. If we confirm a technical fault, we will issue a refund of the affected transaction.

## 4. Statutory consumer rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy overrides any refund or cancellation right you have under applicable law. Specific rights vary by jurisdiction.

### South Africa

Under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2008**, certain refund rights apply to consumer transactions, including remedies where a service is materially defective. We will honour any refund obligations we have under this Act.

### European Union and United Kingdom

If you are a consumer based in the EU or UK, you may have a statutory right to withdraw from a digital purchase within **14 days** of the transaction under the Consumer Rights Directive (and equivalent UK legislation), provided you have not yet started using the digital content.

Because Credits become immediately usable on issuance, this right is typically waived at the point of purchase. By completing your purchase and confirming you wish to receive Credits immediately, you acknowledge that:

- The 14-day withdrawal right does not apply once you start using the Credits
- You expressly consent to immediate delivery of digital content
- You acknowledge you lose your right of withdrawal upon performance

If you have purchased Credits and have not yet used any from that purchase, you may still be eligible to withdraw within 14 days. Contact [hello@voxdex.com](mailto:hello@voxdex.com).

### Other jurisdictions

If your country grants you statutory refund rights stronger than this policy, those rights apply. Contact us with the details of your jurisdiction and we will assess your request accordingly.

## 5. Discretionary refunds

Outside of technical faults and statutory rights, we may issue refunds at our discretion in exceptional circumstances. Examples include:

- A purchase was made by a minor on an account that should not have been registered
- Significant unauthorized use of a payment method (separate from typical fraud claims, which should go to your bank)
- A demonstrable hardship case where we choose to assist

Discretionary refunds are not a right and are granted on a case-by-case basis. We are not obligated to provide them and may decline any request.

## 6. What is not refundable

To be clear, the following are **not refundable** under any circumstances except those listed in Sections 3 and 4:

- Credits that have been used in any prediction, market, vote, or other Service feature
- Credits that have been transferred, gifted, or expired
- Credits awarded as bonuses, promotions, or rewards (these were not paid for)
- Subscription fees for any subscription features (if applicable in future)
- Charges related to suspended or terminated accounts where the suspension was due to a violation of our [Terms of Service](/terms)

## 7. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute through your bank or card issuer instead of contacting us first, we may:

- Suspend or terminate your account pending resolution
- Reverse Credits associated with the disputed transaction
- Decline future purchases from accounts associated with chargeback activity

We strongly encourage you to contact [hello@voxdex.com](mailto:hello@voxdex.com) before initiating a chargeback. Most issues can be resolved directly and quickly.

## 8. How refunds are processed

When a refund is approved:

- It is processed by our **Merchant of Record** (the third-party payment provider that handles transactions on our behalf), not directly by VoxDex
- The refund is issued to the original payment method used for the purchase
- Processing time depends on your bank or card issuer, typically **5–10 business days**
- You will receive an email confirmation when the refund is issued

We do not offer refunds in alternative forms (e.g., other payment methods, store credit, or Credits in lieu of cash).

If the original payment method is no longer available (e.g., the card was cancelled), the Merchant of Record will contact you for alternative arrangements.

## 9. Currency and exchange rates

Refunds are issued in the same currency as the original transaction. If your bank converted the original payment from another currency, you may receive slightly more or less than you originally paid due to exchange-rate fluctuations between the date of purchase and the date of refund. VoxDex is not responsible for currency-conversion differences.

## 10. Account closure and unused Credits

If you **close your VoxDex account voluntarily**, any unused Credits are forfeited and are not refundable. We recommend using your Credits before closing your account.

If we **close your account due to a violation** of our [Terms of Service](/terms), unused Credits are forfeited without refund.

If we **close your account for operational reasons unrelated to any violation by you** (e.g., we discontinue the Service), we will make reasonable efforts to refund unused Credits purchased within the previous 12 months.

Any refund request is governed by the version of this policy in effect at the time of the original purchase.
