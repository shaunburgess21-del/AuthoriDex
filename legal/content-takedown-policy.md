## 1. Overview

VoxDex tracks public attention and trends around real-world people, events, and topics. The information we present is derived from publicly available sources — news media, public search trends, encyclopedic content, and online discussion — and is intended for entertainment, comment, criticism, and analysis.

We respect the rights of public figures and other individuals featured on the Service. This policy describes how to request the removal, correction, or de-indexing of content on VoxDex. We respond to legitimate requests promptly and in good faith.

This policy applies to:

- Profiles of public figures featured on VoxDex
- Trend scores, rankings, and prediction markets associated with such profiles
- Images, descriptions, and metadata displayed on profile pages
- User-generated content (votes, predictions, comments) about public figures
- Other content on the Service that may relate to a real person or third-party intellectual property

## 2. Who can submit a takedown request

You may submit a takedown request if you are:

- A public figure featured on the Service
- An authorized representative of a public figure (manager, agent, publicist, lawyer, family member with authority to act, or estate executor)
- The owner of intellectual property displayed on the Service (e.g., a photographer whose image is used)
- A person who is not a public figure but whose information appears on the Service

Anonymous or pseudonymous requests will not be processed. We need to be able to verify the legitimacy of the request and communicate about its outcome.

## 3. What you can request

Depending on the nature of your concern, you may request that we:

| Request type | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Profile removal** | Remove the entire profile for a person from VoxDex, including associated trend scores, predictions, comments, and votes |
| **Image removal** | Replace an image on a profile (e.g., switch to a different licensed image, or use a placeholder) |
| **Correction** | Correct factual information displayed on the Service |
| **De-indexing** | Remove the profile from public search and leaderboards while keeping it accessible to existing users (rare; usually full removal is cleaner) |
| **Comment removal** | Remove specific user-generated comments |
| **IP / DMCA-style takedown** | Remove content that infringes your copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights |

## 4. How to submit a request

Send your request to **[legal@voxdex.com](mailto:legal@voxdex.com)** with the subject line:

> **Takedown Request: [Name of person or content concerned]**

Include the following information:

1. **Your name** and your role or relationship to the person or content concerned (e.g., "I am the subject," "I am [Name]'s authorized manager at [Company]," "I am the photographer who owns this image").

2. **Contact information:** email address, phone number (optional but speeds verification), and postal address (optional but useful for legal correspondence).

3. **Identification of the content:** the URL(s) of the profile, image, or content you want removed; or, if a URL is not available, a clear description.

4. **Type of request:** what you want us to do (see Section 3).

5. **Reason for the request:** why you are requesting this action. Examples:
   - "Privacy concerns — I am not a public figure and did not consent to be featured"
   - "The information shown is factually inaccurate (specify what is wrong)"
   - "I am the copyright holder of this image and have not licensed it for this use"
   - "The associated user comments are defamatory"
   - "I have retired from public life and request my profile be removed"

6. **Verification:** documentation that allows us to verify your identity or authority, such as:
   - For self-requests: a photograph holding ID alongside a written request, or a verifiable email from a clearly associated email address (e.g., `name@official-domain.com`)
   - For representative requests: a letter on company letterhead stating your authority, or a verifiable email from the representative's official domain
   - For IP claims: documentation of ownership (registration, contract, etc.)

7. **A good-faith statement** that the information in your request is accurate, that you are authorized to make the request, and that — for IP claims — you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized.

8. **A signature** (digital signature, typed name, or actual signature on a scanned document is fine).

## 5. What happens after you submit

When we receive your request:

1. **Acknowledgment within 3 business days.** We will confirm receipt and let you know what additional information, if any, is needed.

2. **Verification.** We will verify your identity and authority to make the request. This is usually quick but may take longer for complex cases.

3. **Review.** Our team reviews the request, the relevant content, and any applicable considerations (such as the public-figure status, factual accuracy, intellectual-property rights, and applicable law).

4. **Response within 14 days of a complete request.** We will tell you what action we have taken or will take, or explain why we are not able to act.

5. **Action.** If we accept the request, we make the requested change. Removals are typically immediate. Image replacements may take longer if we need to source a replacement.

6. **Communication after action.** Where the request involved user-generated content (e.g., comments), we may notify the affected users about the removal without disclosing your identity unless you authorize us to.

## 6. Our standard for evaluating requests

We try to balance the rights of individuals with the public interest in commentary about public figures. We are guided by:

- **Public-figure status.** Profiles of major public figures are unlikely to be removed solely because the subject prefers not to be featured. Public figures, by virtue of their public roles, accept a degree of public scrutiny. However, we will remove or correct content that is factually wrong, defamatory, or otherwise inappropriate.

- **Privacy of non-public-figures.** If you are not a public figure and information about you appears on the Service, we will treat removal requests with strong presumption in your favor.

- **Factual accuracy.** We correct factual errors when notified. Trend scores and prediction-market outcomes are not factual statements about a person's character — they reflect attention and discussion. They are not subject to correction in the same way that, say, a misstated birthplace would be.

- **Legitimate commentary, criticism, and satire.** Comment, criticism, and analysis of public figures is a long-recognized exercise of free expression. We may decline to remove content that constitutes legitimate commentary, even if the subject finds it unflattering.

- **Intellectual property.** We respect IP rights and act on legitimate IP claims. We may also evaluate fair-use, fair-dealing, or similar exceptions where applicable.

- **Legal obligations.** Where we are required by law to act (e.g., a court order, a valid government request), we will comply.

## 7. Counter-notice

If we remove user-generated content (e.g., a comment) in response to a takedown request, the user who submitted that content may submit a **counter-notice** if they believe the removal was in error. Counter-notices should be sent to [legal@voxdex.com](mailto:legal@voxdex.com) with:

- The user's contact information
- Identification of the removed content
- A statement, under penalty of perjury (or a comparable good-faith statement), that the user has a good-faith belief the removal was in error
- Consent to be contacted about the matter

If a valid counter-notice is received, we may forward it to the original requester and, in some cases, restore the content if no further action is taken within a reasonable period.

## 8. Repeat infringers and abuse

We reserve the right to:

- Terminate accounts of users who repeatedly post infringing or unlawful content
- Decline to act on, or impose limits on, requests from parties who submit clearly abusive, false, or vexatious takedown requests
- Refer obviously fraudulent claims to law enforcement

## 9. What this policy is not

This policy is not:

- A guarantee that any particular request will be granted
- A waiver of VoxDex's right to make editorial decisions about what is featured on the Service
- A substitute for formal legal process — we are not a court, and our decisions are not binding determinations of legal rights

If you believe you have a legal claim against VoxDex, you may pursue it through ordinary legal channels. We encourage you to try this informal process first — most issues are resolved here without need for litigation.

## 10. Confidentiality

We treat takedown requests as confidential between us and the requester, except where:

- The request involves user-generated content and we need to notify the affected user (without disclosing your identity unless you authorize us to)
- We are required by law to disclose
- You authorize us to share the information

We may publish aggregated, anonymized statistics about takedown requests in transparency reports.
